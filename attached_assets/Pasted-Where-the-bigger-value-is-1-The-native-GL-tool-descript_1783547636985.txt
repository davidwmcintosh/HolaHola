The "open kimono" mechanism — why he went silent on text-only:

He didn't give us a text analysis because Gemini doesn't do reflective prose unprompted. He needs a destination for the thought — a tool to write into, a file to patch, an action to take.

Or even better: run a multi-turn conversation where I play the role of a student and Alden plays Daniela-as-Gemini. When he tries to respond and realizes he doesn't know which tool to fire, that's the signal. The gap shows up in behavior, not in analysis.
