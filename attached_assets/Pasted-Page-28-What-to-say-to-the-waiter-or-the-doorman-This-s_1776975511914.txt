Page 198: This seems to be about the command form. To give a command in Spanish:
- AR verbs end in the letter E.
- ER and IR verbs end in the letter A.

Examples:
1. Hablar (to speak) → Habla despacio (Speak slowly)
2. Mirar (to look) → Mire (Look)
3. Contestar (to answer) → Conteste el teléfono (Answer the phone)
4. Tomar (to take) → Tome esto (Take this)
5. Vender (to sell) → Vende la casa (Sell the house)
6. Aprender (to learn) → Aprende la lección (Learn the lesson)
7. Escribir (to write) → Escriba (Write)

Pronouns are added to the command to form one word. For example, "Escribir" becomes "Escríbame" (Write me).

Several irregular verbs end in "GA" in the command form:
1. Oír (to hear) → Oiga (Hear)
2. Traer (to bring) → Tráigamelo (Bring it to me)
3. Venir (to come) → Venga acá (Come here)
4. Hacer (to do) → Hágalo (Do it)
5. Decir (to say) → Dígame (Tell me)
