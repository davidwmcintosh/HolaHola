The page ordering is fine for now. We're just trying to get our model down so that we can apply it to other places.

The first thing I'm noticing after Chapter 23 is a header: "Where are you going? Ir: to go." Madrigal does not have any chapter titles or anything; they get right into the content.

Next, I see "Voy (I'm going)" and "al (to the)," but they are on separate lines. They should be on the same line with space between them. I then see the four pictures for "Voy al," which is great, but then I see four pictures for "No voy" in Madrigal at the bottom of page nine.

After the four pictures, we have the questions. It starts with "Voy al hotel," which is the "yo" form, but then it asks questions. It introduces the phrases with "¿Va? (Are you going?)" just like we had "Voy (I'm going)" for the pictures. Now you have:
1. ¿Va al hotel?
2. ¿Va al banco?
3. ¿Va al garaje?
4. ¿Va al restaurante?

All of these are translated into English. There is a note at the bottom saying that in Spanish, you generally drop subject pronouns (I, you, we, they, etc.).

Then you have an introductory sentence again: "No, voy... (No, I'm not going)" and "al (to the)." Then your four pictures. For the "Voy (I'm going)" section, we have:
1. Voy
2. Va
3. Vamos
4. Van
5. No voy
6. No va
7. No vamos
8. No van

The next page goes directly into the same format. At the top of the page, we have "Vamos (let's go)," then "al (to the)," followed by four more pictures. Then "Vamos al hotel" and "Vamos al club." That could be substituted with your little substituter at the bottom. There is also a note at the bottom stating that "vamos" means both "let's go" and "we are going."

Page 12 has "¿Va? (Are you going?)," "al (to the)," "Voy (I'm going)," and "Sí (Yes)." You then have four more pictures with two phrases under each:
1. ¿Va al banco? Sí, voy al banco.
2. ¿Va al parque? Sí, voy al parque.

It gives two more examples without pictures and then ends the page with "Va:":
1. You are going / He is going / She is going / It is going
2. ¿Va?: Are you going? / Is he going? / Is she going? / Is it going?
