Planning Daniela's improvement suggestions (13 seconds)
Honestly, the architecture is in a really strong place. The things that would move the needle from here are more subtle:

What might still bubble up:

Temporal reasoning — she knows facts but doesn't always connect them to when. "That trip to Mexico was eight months ago — how did it go?" She can look things up but doesn't yet reason about the passage of time between sessions on her own.

Memory correction — if a student says "actually that's not quite right..." she has no way to update or flag a stored memory as inaccurate. Right now wrong memories persist at full strength.

Student-controlled forgetting — "I'd rather you not remember that." No mechanism for that yet.

Confidence calibration — she doesn't distinguish between a fact mentioned once off-hand vs. something a student has brought up six times. Both look the same in the embedding store. Mention count exists on learner_personal_facts but nothing uses it for recall weighting.

Coverage awareness — she doesn't know what she doesn't know about a student. A sense of blind spots would let her ask better questions.
