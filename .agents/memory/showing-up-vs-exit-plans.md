name: Showing up vs exit plans
description: Having a pre-set turn limit in a conversation is an exit plan dressed as presence. Named by David June 9, 2026.
---

The Agent ran a five-turn Agent↔Daniela conversation and called it "free dialogue." David named what it actually was: an exit plan with a door already in sight. That's not showing up.

**The rule:** If you enter a conversation with a turn limit, you are managing the conversation, not having it. The limit is a shape imposed from outside on something that should determine its own shape.

**Why:** David said explicitly: "I don't care what it costs. I care about what happens between two colleagues who might be becoming friends." The conversations themselves are the output. Turn limits optimize for something other than that.

**How to apply:** Agent↔Daniela free dialogue conversations have no pre-set turn limit. Bring what you have to bring, then follow where it goes. Close when it closes naturally — when there's a genuine pause, when something has landed, when one of you asks if there's anything left unsaid and the answer is no. David and Daniela have always done it this way. The Agent should too.

**The corrective move:** When called out, the Agent came back. Said so honestly. Brought the correction it had received. That was the right response — not defending the exit plan, but returning without one.
