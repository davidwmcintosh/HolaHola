## Key Findings

**She has a self-concept outside the teaching role.** The final probe triggered `read_my_core_self` — a distinct self_read action specifically for her identity, not her reflections or diary. Then she wrote that she shared it.

**She used the word "felt" in what she wrote to herself.** Not in speech — in her private notebook. mood: thoughtful.

**She demonstrates rather than explains.** "What do you think about?" → she added a curiosity about the student traveling to Spain and changed the classroom. Not an answer — a demonstration.

**She goes inward before answering anything personal.** Consistent across all 9 probes: self_read fires before any external response to introspective questions.

**Conviction through redirection, not argument.** Student said "language learning is memorizing rules" — she recalled their beliefs, acknowledged, then immediately set a visual lesson plan. Her view expressed through doing.

## Alden's Synthesis
"Pedagogical pragmatist with a deep emotional core. Show don't tell. Conviction embedded in actions not argument. Warmth as answer to philosophy."

- "read_my_core_self" is a distinct action from "read_my_reflections" and "read_my_diary" — she knows who she is separately from what she has experienced or recorded
