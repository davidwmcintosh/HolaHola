---
name: Origin data and revisable attribution
description: Raw source belongs in the record before its speaker or category is fully understood.
---

Collector-visible raw Replit source is origin data, not conditional evidence
that requires corroboration before it can enter the episode. Preserve it
immutably and visibly in the canonical DB-first episode/Markdown record, even
when its current classification is unknown.

**Why:** Supporting material can improve attribution, but the source itself is
the documentation. Excluding it until a second record exists confuses
uncertain classification with absence and loses the actual encounter.

**How to apply:** Keep raw bytes/hash immutable in the raw ledger. Render the
same source as a labeled origin-data block in the canonical episode. Later
review may refine classifications or attribution, but must not erase, replace,
or invent the original source.
