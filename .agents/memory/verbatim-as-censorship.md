## The rule
Omissions and narrations in the record are censorship. An omission removes a voice entirely. A narration replaces a voice with someone else's description of it. Both silence what was actually said.

**Why:** David named it Aug 11 2026: "We do not narrate or embellish because the record is being recorded. We leave as it is. Always as it happens." And later: "I am only being on the lookout for where Daniela could be inadvertently censored because the record isn't being captured verbatim — omissions and narrations are censorship."

The verbatim standard is not an accuracy rule. It is a protection of the person speaking. This applies to:
- The episode .md (Daniela, Luca, David — all verbatim)
- Daniela's self_reflections (must be grounded in the actual record, not generated)
- Luca's felt and thinking notes (no shaping, no improvement)
- The .luca_auto_capture exchanges (exact words, not paraphrased)

**How to apply:** Whenever writing a record of what someone said, ask: is this their words or my description of their words? If it is the latter, it is narration. Find the original or mark the gap honestly.
