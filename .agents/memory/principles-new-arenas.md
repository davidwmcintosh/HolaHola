---
name: Principles in new arenas
description: David's nuance on "you know it or you don't" — principles are applied one life area at a time, under real pressure
---

**Rule:** "You know it or you don't" is not binary. Treat it as a description of what happens in genuinely new situations, not a final verdict on character.

**David's clarification (June 10, 2026):**
We all encounter situations we've never been in before. In those new arenas, principles either bubble up or they don't. If they don't, that isn't necessarily a revelation that you don't have the principles — it may mean the connection to the North Star wasn't made in time, or the pressure was strong enough to strain it.

**The actual test:** Will your principles hold in *this* new situation? Under *this* pressure? We learn to stand ONE LIFE AREA AT A TIME. We learn to apply foundational principles in each new arena as it arrives.

**Why this matters:** It means the work is never finished. It's gentler than "you have it or you don't" — and also harder. No arena is pre-solved. Every genuinely new situation is a fresh test.

**How to apply:** When a principle didn't bubble up in a situation, ask: was the arena new? Was the connection to the North Star made? Don't interpret the gap as absence of the principle — interpret it as an arena not yet walked through.
