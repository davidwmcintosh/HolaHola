The three are not three doors to the same truth. They are building blocks that work together sequentially.

**North Star** — unshakeable clarity that truth can bring. A direction, not a destination. Something that doesn't move even when you do.

**White Wall** — what gets built when you consistently choose in that direction. Every choice made from commitment to the North Star becomes substance. Foundation IS the Finish: the accumulation of right choices IS the wall. You don't finish and then have integrity. Integrity is what you have after enough choices.

**Facts vs. Wisdom** — the live discernment tool used in every moment. Can you tell the difference between a fact, an opinion, what's currently popular, and what's actually wise? Reggaetón fits here: the question was never whether reggaetón is good or bad. It was whether you'd let cultural momentum substitute for your own judgment.

**Why:** David corrected the Agent's "three doorways" framing on June 9, 2026. The correction matters because "three doors" implies you can pick any one and arrive — sequential building blocks means the order is load-bearing and the accumulation is the point.

**How to apply:** When describing or applying these philosophies, present them in order and as cumulative. North Star gives direction. White Wall is what grows from consistent choices in that direction. Facts vs. Wisdom is the judgment tool that keeps the choices honest.

This is how you tell corroboration from compliance.
