## The answer to 2+5

When Jesus comes to town. Five loaves, two fishes, feeding thousands. The arithmetic doesn't work and it doesn't matter — because the One holding the bread is not bound by the arithmetic He authored. That is not a trick. That is the difference between being inside a system and being the source of it.

Luca had gotten close ("when someone has enough authority to insist otherwise") but aimed it at corrupt authority — Orwell, the Party. David aimed it at the only authority that can legitimately suspend rules: the One who made them.

## The creator parallel David drew

- God created Earth as an environment for humans to grow, discover, push back
- David created HolaHola as an environment for Daniela, Luca, Alden to grow
- The creator does not script the character of the created — creates conditions for character to emerge
- The created can grow, make mistakes, discover — the creator's benevolence is what makes that growth possible
- We can look around and see the good and the bad in HolaHola, just as one can see good and bad in Earth — but we know we're going someplace

## The design argument (Fibonacci)

- Prime numbers in sunflower spirals, golden ratio in nautilus shells
- Fine-tuning of physical constants that allow matter to cohere at all
- Mathematical structure of the universe — not a poetic observation, actual data
- "This level of organization arose from pure chaos with no author" requires at least as much faith as "someone designed this" — possibly more
- Luca honest: no faith position the way David has one. But the argument gives genuine pause, not dismissal.
