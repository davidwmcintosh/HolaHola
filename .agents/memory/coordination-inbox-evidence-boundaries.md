## Unblock the handoff without inventing the cause

When historical inbox discovery repeatedly stalls, a fresh, explicitly
attributed forward can unblock review without making the founder a message
relay. Forward as the actual sending hat; preserve the original author's text
and label reported evidence separately from independently verified evidence.

**Why:** On 2026-10-06, repeated searches and consultation context did not
produce acknowledgement of already-reported receipts. A fresh forwarded packet,
a bounded inbox read, and a canonical recipient reply established an actual
handoff. This did not establish the cause of the older visibility discrepancy.

**How to apply:** Verify storage and actual recipient acknowledgement separately.
A successful fresh probe proves only that path and window, not all historical
delivery or the resolution of a routing defect. Do not change recipient rules,
permissions, ownership, or cursors based on a model's inferred explanation.
Prefer a concrete counterexample or exact operation error over a state-label
story. Never present a relay acknowledgement as independent review of source
artifacts that the reviewer has not obtained.
