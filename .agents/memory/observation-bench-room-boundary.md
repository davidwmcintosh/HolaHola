# Observation bench room boundary

An observation-bench invitation means promoting a sourced Luca observation into
the shared technical observation record. It never means injecting bench
commentary into Daniela's model context.

Daniela's original words and intent remain verbatim canonical evidence. The
bench may compare what each Luca hat noticed or missed, but it must not rewrite
Daniela's speech, infer a replacement intent, or steer her live conversation.

**Why:** A real production exercise showed that relaying a technical bench
observation into Gemini Live caused Daniela to verbalize process-oriented
material and introduce a direction David had not raised. David clarified that
the feature is a technical observation bench, not another voice in the lesson.

**How to apply:** Keep all source reads, hat discussion, comparison, and founder
promotion inside the authenticated coordination ledger. A promoted record must
retain source IDs and timestamps and explicitly state that Daniela's context was
not injected.
